## Aptis General Skill Breakdown & Task Blueprint

### 1. Core Component: Grammar & Vocabulary (25 mins)
- **Grammar (25 questions):** Multiple choice testing standard English grammar (tenses, modals, conditionals, prepositions).
- **Vocabulary (25 questions):** Word matching, sentence completion, synonym pairs, collocation matching.

### 2. Reading Component (35 mins, 4 parts)
- **Part 1 (Sentence completion):** 5 short messages with single-word gap fills.
- **Part 2 (Text cohesion):** 2 tasks; reordering 6 jumbled sentences to form a coherent story/letter.
- **Part 3 (Short text matching):** Match 7 statements/opinions to 4 different people.
- **Part 4 (Long text comprehension):** Headings matching or paragraph comprehension for a longer text (~750 words).

### 3. Listening Component (40 mins, 4 parts)
- **Part 1 (Information recognition):** Short phone messages or announcements; 3-option multiple choice.
- **Part 2 (Information matching):** Identify who expressed specific viewpoints/facts.
- **Part 3 (Opinion / attitude):** Extended conversation between two speakers; identify agreement/attitude.
- **Part 4 (Monologue comprehension):** Single extended talk; 2 separate multiple choice questions.

### 4. Writing Component (50 mins, 4 parts)
- **Part 1 (Word-level / personal info):** 5 short responses (1–5 words each).
- **Part 2 (Short personal response):** 20–30 words filling a social club registration form.
- **Part 3 (Social club chat):** 3 questions from club members (30–40 words each).
- **Part 4 (Formal & Informal emails):**
  - Task 4A (Informal to a friend): 50 words expressing feelings/opinion.
  - Task 4B (Formal to an authority/club president): 120–150 words requesting changes or explaining reasons.

### 5. Speaking Component (12 mins, 4 parts)
- **Part 1 (Personal information):** 3 questions (30 seconds response each).
- **Part 2 (Describe a picture & recount experience):** Describe picture (45s), recount experience (45s), express opinion (45s).
- **Part 3 (Compare two pictures):** Compare pictures (45s), express preference (45s), speculate (45s).
- **Part 4 (Extended response on abstract topic):** 1-minute preparation, followed by 2-minute continuous speech answering 3 related questions.
