# ✍️ Bài Mẫu Writing Aptis B2 — Model Answers & Phân Tích Chấm Điểm

## 1. Part 4 — Informal Email (Gửi Bạn Thân: 50 từ)

**Tình huống:** CLB thông báo tăng phí sinh hoạt đột ngột.
**Bài mẫu đạt chuẩn B2:**
> "Hi Sam,

Have you heard about the club's sudden fee increase? I'm honestly shocked because we already pay enough, and they haven't upgraded any equipment. I think we should talk to the committee together before next week. What do you think?

Catch you later,
Alex"

**Phân tích tiêu chí:**
- **Độ dài:** 48 từ (hoàn hảo trong khoảng 45-55 từ).
- **Ngữ vực (Register):** Tự nhiên, thân mật, dùng từ viết tắt (`I'm`, `haven't`), lời chào/kết thân thiện (`Hi`, `Catch you later`).
- **Nội dung:** Bày tỏ cảm xúc và đề xuất phương án với bạn.

---

## 2. Part 4 — Formal Email (Gửi Ban Quản Lý: 120-150 từ)

**Bài mẫu đạt chuẩn B2/C1:**
> "Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing with reference to the recent announcement regarding the increase in annual membership fees.

While I appreciate that operational costs may have risen, this unexpected decision has caused considerable concern among several members, including myself. Many students and part-time workers who participate in our weekly sessions might find this significant price adjustment difficult to afford.

To resolve this issue constructively, I would like to propose that the management consider offering a discounted rate for long-standing members or introducing a monthly instalment scheme. This would provide greater financial flexibility while maintaining club revenues.

Thank you for taking my suggestions into consideration. I look forward to receiving your response.

Yours faithfully,
Alexander Nguyen"

**Phân tích tiêu chí:**
- **Độ dài:** 132 từ (chuẩn 120-150 từ).
- **Cấu trúc 4 đoạn:** Purpose $\rightarrow$ Impact & Concern $\rightarrow$ Constructive Solutions $\rightarrow$ Polite Sign-off.
- **Register:** Chuẩn mực 100%, không viết tắt, dùng câu bị động và liên từ trang trọng (`While`, `To resolve this issue`, `Yours faithfully`).
