## 3. Part-by-Part Specifications

### 3.1 Grammar & Vocabulary (Mandatory Core Component)
- **Status:** `[VERIFIED]`
- **Official Duration:** 25 minutes
- **Total Questions:** 50 items (Scale 0–50)
- **Structure:**
  - **Part 1 (Grammar - 25 questions):** `[VERIFIED]` 3-option multiple choice sentences testing standard written English syntax (tenses, modals, conditionals, prepositions, determiners, gerunds/infinitives).
  - **Part 2 (Vocabulary - 25 questions):** `[VERIFIED]` 5 distinct question sets (5 items per set) matching target words against a shared bank of **10 options**:
    - Set 1: Word matching / Synonyms
    - Set 2: Word definitions
    - Set 3: Sentence completion / Word usage
    - Set 4: Collocations / Word combinations
    - Set 5: Phrasal verbs / Category associations

---

### 3.2 Reading Component
- **Status:** `[VERIFIED]`
- **Official Duration:** 35 minutes (4 parts, 25 items total)
- **Structure:**
  - **Part 1 (Sentence Completion):** `[VERIFIED]` Short informal email/note with 5 blanks (3 drop-down options per gap).
  - **Part 2 (Text Cohesion):** `[VERIFIED]` 2 stories/letters. In each story, Sentence 1 is fixed as an anchor; candidate orders the remaining 5 sentences.
  - **Part 3 (Opinion Matching):** `[VERIFIED]` 4 biography/opinion passages (Persons A, B, C, D); candidate matches 7 statements to the respective person (many-to-one matching allowed).
  - **Part 4 (Matching Headings):** `[VERIFIED]` Long text (~750 words) with 7 paragraphs; candidate assigns 7 headings from a pool of 8 headings (1 extra distractor).

---

### 3.3 Listening Component
- **Status:** `[VERIFIED]`
- **Official Duration:** 40 minutes (4 parts, 17 tasks / up to 20 recordings)
- **Playback Rule:** `[VERIFIED]` Candidates are permitted to listen to each recording **twice** (`maxPlays: 2`).
- **Structure:**
  - **Part 1 (Information Recognition):** `[VERIFIED]` Short monologue/dialogue recordings with 1 multiple choice question (3 options) per recording.
  - **Part 2 (Speaker Information Matching):** `[VERIFIED]` 4 short monologues on a shared topic; match 4 speakers to 4 statements from a pool of 6 options.
  - **Part 3 (Opinion / Discussion):** `[VERIFIED]` 1 conversation between a Man and a Woman; identify who expresses 4 specific opinions (`Man`, `Woman`, or `Both`).
  - **Part 4 (Extended Monologues):** `[VERIFIED]` 2 separate longer monologues with 2 multiple choice questions each (main idea and speaker attitude).

---

### 3.4 Writing Component
- **Status:** `[VERIFIED]`
- **Official Duration:** 50 minutes (4 parts)
- **Sub-part Timing Recommendation:** `[PROJECT DESIGN DECISION]` (Part 1: 3 mins, Part 2: 7 mins, Part 3: 10 mins, Part 4: 30 mins).
- **Structure & Word Count Audit:**
  - **Part 1 (Personal Form):** `[VERIFIED]` 5 short prompt questions. Official guidance: 1–5 words per item.
  - **Part 2 (Short Personal Text):** `[VERIFIED]` 1 interest/registration prompt in full sentences. Official guidance: **20–30 words**.
  - **Part 3 (Social Network Chat):** `[VERIFIED]` 3 questions from different club members. Official guidance: **around 40 words** for each response. Project validation rule allows flexible tolerance (e.g. 30–50 words).
  - **Part 4 (Email Writing):** `[VERIFIED]`
    - **Task 4A (Informal Email):** Official guidance: **40–50 words** (express feelings and state preference).
    - **Task 4B (Formal Email):** Official guidance: **120–150 words** (polite structure, alternative proposals, formal register).

---

### 3.5 Speaking Component
- **Status:** `[VERIFIED]`
- **Official Duration:** ~12 minutes (4 parts)
- **Structure:**
  - **Part 1 (Personal Info):** `[VERIFIED]` 3 personal questions (0s preparation, **30 seconds** response each).
  - **Part 2 (1 Picture Description & Discussion):** `[VERIFIED]` 1 photo, **3 distinct questions** (0s preparation, **45 seconds** response each):
    1. Describe the photograph.
    2. Talk about a personal experience related to the topic.
    3. Express an opinion on the topic.
  - **Part 3 (2 Pictures Comparison & Discussion):** `[VERIFIED]` 2 contrasting photos, **3 distinct questions** (0s preparation, **45 seconds** response each):
    1. Describe and compare the two photos.
    2. Speculate on a situation or question related to the photos.
    3. Give an opinion or express a personal preference.
  - **Part 4 (Abstract Topic Extended Talk):** `[VERIFIED]` 1 topic card with 3 interrelated questions (**60 seconds** preparation, **120 seconds** continuous response answering all 3 questions).
